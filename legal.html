Legal Policies & Terms

Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines for the Faith Nexus 2027 Summit and 2026 Soft Launch.

Last updated: August 2026 · World Christian Content Creators Summit (WCCCS)

Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering for, applying for passes, or attending Faith Nexus 2027 (including the Hybrid Soft Launch on 29 October 2026 and the Main Summit from 14–16 April 2027 in Johannesburg, South Africa), you agree to abide by these Terms of Service.

2. Registration & Delegate Access

Registration of interest and delegate pass applications are processed through official Faith Nexus channels. Approved passes grant entry to designated plenaries, masterclasses, and networking zones. Passes may not be transferred or resold without prior written authorization from the convener.

3. Intellectual Property

All materials, frameworks (including the 7 Pillars Framework), presentations, and digital broadcasts produced under Faith Nexus remain the protected intellectual property of WCCCS and respective rights holders. Unauthorized recording, commercial rebroadcasting, or distribution is prohibited.

Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

We collect essential delegate information including full name, institutional affiliation, email address, sector classification, and travel logistics strictly for event orchestration, accreditation, and communication.

2. Data Stewardship

Your data is stewarded with strict confidentiality according to South African POPIA and international data protection standards. We do not sell or lease personal data to third parties.

3. Media & Broadcast Consent

By attending Faith Nexus 2027 in person or virtually, delegates acknowledge that photography, video recording, and audio broadcasts will take place for archival, educational, and global streaming purposes.

Community & Convening Standards

Faith Nexus is a Kingdom-aligned, high-conviction summit uniting leaders from diverse nations and traditions. We require all delegates, exhibitors, and speakers to maintain mutual honor, professional integrity, and constructive engagement throughout all sessions.
