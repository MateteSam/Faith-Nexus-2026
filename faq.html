Frequently Asked Questions

Who should attend Faith Nexus 2026?

Faith Nexus is designed for pastors, media professionals, digital creators, technologists, and ministry leaders who are passionate about shaping culture through Kingdom-centric content and innovation.

What is the difference between Plenary and Full Access?

Plenary Access provides entry to all main sessions and the exhibition. Full Access (Premium) includes everything in Plenary plus priority booking for Creator Masterclasses and the Nexus Studio, and preferential access to the FN100 Gala.

How does the Global Virtual Activation work?

The Virtual Activation on 2nd May 2026 is a free global digital broadcast. Registration will open separately, providing access to curated sessions and TalkWorld digital resources.

Are group discounts available?

Yes, we offer specialized rates for church delegations and organizations of 10 or more people. Please enquire via our Registration hub or email hospitality@faithnexus.digital.
